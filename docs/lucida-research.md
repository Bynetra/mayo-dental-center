   - In-clinic chairside blue LED whitening lifting shades up to 6–8 tones, paired with desensitizing fluoride varnish and custom home trays.

   - Ultra-thin custom ceramic and E-max laminates for aesthetic smile makeovers.

   - Discreet transparent orthodontic aligners (Eon Aligners / invisible aligners) as well as traditional braces for smile realignment.
6. **Advanced Microscopic Endodontics (Root Canal Therapy):**
   - Headed by Endodontic Specialist Dr. Adey Al-Shaar. Electronic apex location, rotary instrumentation, biocompatible warm gutta-percha obturation.
7. **Permanent Dental Implants:**
   - Grade 4 biocompatible titanium implant fixtures, surgical guide placement, customized porcelain abutments and crowns.
8. **Fluoride Remineralization & Preventive Dentistry:**
   - Medical topical fluoride application, fissure sealants for children, enamel erosion management.
9. **Tooth Extractions & Gentle Oral Surgery:**
   - Atraumatic tooth removal, impacted wisdom teeth management, bone socket preservation.
10. **Emergency Dental Appointments:**
    - Fast triage for acute pulpitis, knocked-out teeth, broken restorations, facial swelling, led by Dr. Mohamed Isa.
